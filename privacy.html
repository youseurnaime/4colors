Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

4colors ("the App," "we," "us") is a puzzle coloring game. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it's used, and your choices — in plain terms, matching exactly what the App actually does.

Information we collect

4colors has no account system, no sign-up, and no login. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts, or location.

Information stored on your device

The App stores a small amount of gameplay data locally on your device only:

  • Progress data (levels completed, error count, time taken, XP earned, and when each level was completed)
  • Tutorial status (whether you've seen the how-to-play screen)
  • Your light/dark appearance preference

This data is never transmitted to us, is not linked to your identity, and is automatically deleted if you uninstall the App. We have no servers and no backend — there is nowhere for this data to go besides your own device.

Third-party services

Advertising (Google AdMob)

4colors shows banner and interstitial ads via Google's Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To serve and measure these ads, Google's SDK may collect and process:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (such as Apple's IDFA, only with your permission)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
  • General device and app information (OS version, device model)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy.

On iOS, the App will ask for your permission (App Tracking Transparency) before using your device's advertising identifier for personalized ads. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they may be less relevant to you.

Your choices:

  • Limit ad tracking via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  • Reset your advertising identifier in your device settings
  • Review Google's ad personalization settings

Children's privacy

4colors is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using the details below and we will take steps to delete it.

Data security

Because the App does not collect personal information or operate any servers, there is no central database of user data that could be exposed in a breach. All gameplay data stays on your device, under your control, protected by your device's own security.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA / similar laws)

Since 4colors itself does not collect or process personal data, most data-subject rights (access, deletion, portability) do not apply to information held by us, as we simply don't hold any. Requests concerning data collected by Google's advertising SDK should be directed to Google via the link above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App or applicable law. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.
