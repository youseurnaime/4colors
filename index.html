4colors

Four colors, zero clashes.

4colors is a relaxing puzzle game built around a simple rule: fill in every region on the board so that no two touching areas ever share the same color. Each puzzle is generated fresh, with a hand-picked palette and a soft, painterly look.

Solve at your own pace, or chase speed and streak bonuses to level up faster. Puzzles get progressively more intricate as you play, so there's always a new layout to figure out.
